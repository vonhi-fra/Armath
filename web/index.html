      Facts

      Small facts should be recalled, not worked out. Each session asks the facts that are due, adds a few new ones, and brings back the ones you miss. A fact is mastered once you've answered it quickly several times on different days.

      Tricks

      Faster ways to do arithmetic in your head. Each drill starts with the trick on its own, then mixes in similar problems so you learn when it applies.
